Someone who finally cleared up their skin after years of feeling uncomfortable in it.

Being able to afford nutritious food each day. I may not get takeout each week but I have learnt to appreciate the fact I can have out of season fruit all year round and be able to afford it.

My boyfriend’s flat that I’ll be moving into that has an old fridge, concrete floors, and hand-me-down furniture but we will be happy and comfortable.
